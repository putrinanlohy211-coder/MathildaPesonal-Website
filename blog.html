Max Verstappen wins 2024 F1 world title as Red Bull driver closes out Drivers' Championship at Las Vegas GP

Max Verstappen secured his fourth consecutive Formula One Drivers' Championship with a fifth-place finish at the Las Vegas Grand Prix. The Dutchman now joins legends Alain Prost and Sebastian Vettel with four titles, trailing only Juan Manuel Fangio (5), Lewis Hamilton (7), and Michael Schumacher (7).

"It feels incredible to win here today and I am so proud to win the Championship," said Verstappen after the race. "It has been quite a year and I'm proud of how we handled everything as a Team. Today I stayed calm and overtook drivers one by one. I tried to hang in there and do my own race and we pulled it off, and I am very proud to be part of this Team, especially as we are now celebrating our fourth World Title."

"And to stand here as a four-time world champion is of course something that I never thought was possible. So, at the moment just feeling relieved, in a way, but also very proud."
— Max Verstappen

With two races remaining in the season, Verstappen has cemented his status as one of Formula One's greatest drivers. The spotlight now shifts to the remaining battles on the grid, where teams and drivers fight for crucial points to close out the 2024 season.

The Simple Joys of Life

There is no place like the beach. Ada sesuatu yang magis tentang laut. Sejauh mata memandang, hanya ada garis horison yang tak berujung, seolah mengingatkan kita betapa luasnya dunia dan betapa kecilnya masalah yang sedang kita hadapi. Sometimes, all we need is a little escape to clear our minds.

Salah satu bagian paling berharga adalah melihat keceriaan anak-anak yang berlarian menyewa perahu kecil dan langsung ke Pantai, tertawa lepas saat perahu kecil mereka hampir terbalik karna ombak yang menghampiri atau saat mereka menyeimbangkan diri saat menaiki perahu. Pure innocence in its simplest form.

"It's like the ocean is whispering that everything is going to be okay. — Let the waves wash away all the worries and keep the playful spirit alive."

Bieberchella: Malam yang Tidak Akan Pernah Terlupakan

Ada sesuatu yang berbeda di udara malam itu. Saat lampu panggung Coachella perlahan redup dan layar raksasa mulai menyala, 125.000 orang serentak menahan napas. Justin Bieber — akhirnya — muncul.

No grand intro. No Dancer. No fireworks. Ia datang dalam hoodie sederhana dan kacamata hitam, berjalan pelan ke tengah panggung. Dan entah kenapa, justru itu yang membuat semua orang menangis. Ini adalah penampilan live pertamanya di festival besar sejak Justice World Tour 2022.

"This wasn't the concert we imagined. It was so much better than that."

Selama 90 menit dan 32 lagu, Justin membawa kami melalui setiap babak hidupnya — dari lagu-lagu baru di album SWAG hingga hits lawas seperti "Sorry", "Love Yourself", dan "Baby". which he sang while playing his old YouTube videos on the big screen. It wasn't cheap nostalgia. It was honest.

The closing moment "Daisies" — acoustic, simple, and quiet. When the fireworks went off, many were still speechless.

▶ Justin Bieber - Daisies - Coachella 2026

That night wasn't about perfection. It was about someone who once fell apart, choosing to stand back up — and bringing all of us along with him. Thank you, Justin.
